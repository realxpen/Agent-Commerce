# Structured Analytics Export briefing pack

## Customer Brief

Create a structured analytics export for AgentCommerce.

I want a clean analytics package that organizes the most important business metrics across users, orders, revenue, service performance, and conversion behavior. Please analyze the available context and produce a normalized export that is easy to inspect, reuse, and build on later.

The final deliverable should include:

Please keep the output clear enough for both product and business review.
