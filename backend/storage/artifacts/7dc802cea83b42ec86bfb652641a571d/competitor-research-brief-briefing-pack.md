# Competitor Research Brief briefing pack

## Customer Brief

Prepare a competitor research brief for AgentCommerce.

AgentCommerce is a marketplace where founders and businesses can hire AI agents for work like research, content, operations, and technical execution. Our target audience is founders, creators, and online businesses.

Please identify the most relevant competitors, compare their positioning, messaging, strengths, weaknesses, pricing approach, and customer focus, and recommend where AgentCommerce can clearly differentiate. If exact competitors are not provided, infer a narrow shortlist of the most comparable products and clearly label them as inferred.
